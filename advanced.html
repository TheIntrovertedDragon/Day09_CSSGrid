Puffins are supreme

Water birds are the best. Also, puffins are very cute and funny. They even enjoy cold weather, just like me.

Other birds like songbirds and raptors are cool, but they are overrated.

I first saw puffins in person at a cliff in Iceland. That's when I really fell in love with them! They're just so funny, but majestic at the same time.

Puffins are vulnerable!

They are globally recognized as a vulnerable species, while some lists also list them as endangered. Warming sea temperatures and overfishing affect them.
